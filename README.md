# 🚨 The Problem

> ### **The Civic Trust Paradox:** "Citizens feel their complaints vanish into a bureaucratic black hole, while honest municipal officers perform grueling ground-work but receive subjective appraisals and zero public recognition."

### ❌ **TRADITIONAL GOVERNANCE :** Open-ended complaints + Manual Audits = Civic Apathy
### ✅ **JANSETU FRAMEWORK :** Closed-Loop Tracking + AI Verification = Institutional Trust

| 😴 The Old Broken Way | ⚡ The JanSetu Standard |
| :--- | :--- |
| ### **The Complaint Black Hole:**<br/>Citizens report issues on outdated portals and never receive updates on whether the work was actually done. | ### **Closed-Loop Awaaz Radar:**<br/>Citizens track issues in real-time, vote (Anumodan) to prioritize them, and get notified upon resolution. |
| ### **Fraudulent Contractor Proofs:**<br/>Contractors submit recycled photos or inflate material bills to siphon municipal budgets. | ### **Pramaan AI Audit:**<br/>Autonomous AI cross-checks GPS metadata, hardware timestamps, image tampering (ELA), and cost benchmarks. |
| ### **Subjective Officer Appraisals:**<br/>Promotions and ward budgets are decided by office politics and paperwork. | ### **Seva Coins & Public Ledger:**<br/>Immutable, objective performance credit that translates to faster appraisals and unlocked ward funding. |

### **JanSetu** ("The People's Bridge") is a decentralized civic accountability network. 

### By combining community prioritization (*Awaaz*) with tamper-proof evidence submission (*Saboot*) and autonomous neural auditing (*Pramaan*), JanSetu ensures that every municipal rupee is spent honestly and every honest officer is objectively rewarded.

---

# 🔥 Key Features

* ### 📢 **Awaaz Feed & Anumodan:** A democratic, Reddit-style feed where citizens post local hazards and the community upvotes (Anumodan) the most critical issues to prioritize municipal response.
* ### 📸 **Tamper-Proof Saboot (Proof of Work):** Officers upload Before/After photos and cost receipts. The system auto-captures hardware NTP timestamps and GPS coordinates to prevent spoofing.
* ### 🤖 **Pramaan AI Verification Agent:** An autonomous audit engine that runs Error Level Analysis (ELA) for image tampering, checks spatial metadata, and compares claimed costs against standard PWD benchmarks.
* ### 🪙 **Seva Coins & Public Ledger:** A non-speculative civic credit system. Officers earn Seva Coins for AI-approved resolutions, translating to objective career appraisals and unlocked discretionary budgets.
* ### 📊 **Open Budget Transparency:** Public-facing data visualization showing category-wise municipal capital expenditure (Roads, Water, Lighting) to maintain total financial clarity.

# 🧠 Architecture

### **Multi-Stage Accountability Pipeline:**
1. ### **Target Extraction:** Citizens submit multi-modal data (text, category, geolocation, images) via the Awaaz Feed.
2. ### **Workflow Routing:** High-Anumodan (highly voted) issues surface on the Officer Task Board for immediate claiming.
3. ### **Multi-Vector AI Analysis:** The Pramaan agent evaluates contractor receipts, cross-references hardware timestamps, and runs computer vision checks on before/after proofs.
4. ### **Credit Allocation:** Generates transparent, tamper-proof performance analytics (Seva Coins) for officers and municipal tax-rebate credits for reporting citizens.

# 🛡️ Responsible AI & Ethical Bounds

### **Core Design Philosophy:** JanSetu is engineered to build institutional trust, not to micromanage or surveil.

* ### 🟢 **Intended Scope:** Celebrating high-impact municipal officers, ensuring transparent allocation of tax rupees, and giving citizens a verifiable voice.
* ### 🔴 **Explicit Boundaries:** Seva Coins are strictly non-speculative and cannot be traded or converted to fiat cash to prevent anti-bribery violations. The AI (Pramaan) never executes punitive disciplinary action; low-confidence scores simply escrow the funds for human vigilance review.
